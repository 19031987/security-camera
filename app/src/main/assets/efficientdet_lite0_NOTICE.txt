efficientdet_lite0.tflite

Object-detection model "EfficientDet-Lite0" (int8), used on the device to say what moved
in a picture (person, animal, vehicle). Nothing is sent off the device.

Source: Google MediaPipe model collection
        https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/object_detector/efficientdet_lite0/int8/latest/efficientdet_lite0.tflite
