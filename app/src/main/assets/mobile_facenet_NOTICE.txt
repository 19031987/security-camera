mobile_facenet.tflite

Face-embedding model "MobileFaceNet", used on the device to recognise enrolled
household members. Nothing is sent off the device.

Source: Qualcomm AI Hub Models, release v0.66.0 (TFLite, float precision)
        https://huggingface.co/qualcomm/MobileFaceNet
